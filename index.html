How these estimates work

Plastic share is calculated only from fibre percentages you enter. Longevity uses fibre mix, condition, care difficulty and construction rating. Cost per wear divides your price by logged or projected wears. Price and brand are not environmental or quality scores. Brand names are recorded, but no unsupported brand quality score is invented.
